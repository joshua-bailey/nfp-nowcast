The revision wedge

First prints have been systematically revised down as QCEW benchmarks arrive. The QCEW early-benchmark model currently implies -58k/month, so the June headline of +128k maps to an implied underlying pace near +70k.

Honest skill: the signal is real (it matches the BLS −911k 2025 preliminary benchmark), but it is contemporaneous; the real-time correlation at the lag the model faces is ~0.78 (6-month QCEW lag), not the 0.93 contemporaneous figure. Effective sample is ~one observation per benchmark cycle.
