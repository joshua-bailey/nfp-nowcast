How wide the uncertainty really is

Bands are empirical, from the model’s own recent out-of-sample errors (60 of them), not from disagreement between models. The 90% band spans 374k. Nowcasting payrolls two days out is genuinely imprecise, and a narrower-looking number would be misleading.

Recent first prints, and where this month sits

Each bar is the monthly change as first published. Later revisions have run persistently downward, so this series reads stronger than the current revised data for the same months. The model forecasts the first print, which is the number that moves markets on the day.

What the individual models say

The published figure is a weighted combination of model families, not any single model. Spread across models is wide this month (-23k to +244k), which is itself a signal that the month is hard to read. ⚠ marks a model that reported a numerical warning; family medians limit how far any one of them can pull the result.

What goes into the forecast

InputWhat it tells the model
Weekly jobless claims
3 series · weekly		New and continuing claims for unemployment benefit — the fastest read on layoffs.
ADP private payrolls
2 series · weekly + monthly		A payroll processor's own count of private employment, out two days before BLS.
Regional Fed business surveys
5 series · monthly		Firms in the Philadelphia, New York and Dallas Fed districts report whether they are hiring, covering both manufacturing and services.
JOLTS labour-market flows
4 series · monthly		Job openings, hires, quits and layoffs. Published with a lag, so it sets the trend rather than the current month.
Weekly Economic Index
1 series · weekly		A broad weekly gauge of US activity, used as general economic context.
Related BLS series
2 series · monthly		Temporary-help employment and the unemployment rate. These arrive with the payroll report itself, so only earlier months are ever used.

Everything above is public data from FRED, the BLS and the regional Federal Reserve banks, with one exception: when a public feed is slow to update we take that same series from our Haver subscription so the forecast is not held up. Alongside these inputs the models use the payroll history itself, always as it stood on the day — never the later revised figures — so the model is never tested on information it could not have had at the time.
